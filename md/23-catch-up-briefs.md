# Brief Anyone on a Messy Project (P16)

<p class="tagline">The full picture in one ask. Added Oct 10, 2026.</p>

> Bring [PERSON] up to speed on [PROJECT]. Look through my email, calendar, and any connected tools, newest first. Start with a three-sentence bottom line: what's true, what's blocked, and who has the next move. Then give me a timeline of who was waiting on whom, a who's-who, open items with an owner and how long each has been sitting, and a list of what the record can't tell us. Leave out anything private that [PERSON] shouldn't see, and tell me what you left out.

Read the brief yourself before it goes to anyone. In the starter kit this ships as the `bring-up-to-speed` skill: ask for it in plain words.
