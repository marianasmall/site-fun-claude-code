# Free Live Claude Workshops and Meetups (P19)

<p class="tagline">The best hands-on training I've found. Emailed Oct 11, 2026. Added Oct 14, 2026.</p>

- **Online (Anthropic and Tenex):** free, live, 90 minutes. A Claude Workshop (Cowork, no coding) and a Claude Code Workshop. Sign up at anthropic.com/events/build-with-claude, filter to Virtual, click Apply.
- **In person (Claude Community):** free meetups worldwide at luma.com/claudecommunity. Register early; popular ones go to a waitlist.

**Three habits you can use right away**

1. Give Claude a briefing it reads every time (Project instructions in Claude, a CLAUDE.md file in Claude Code), including a map of where your things live.
2. One chat per task. Long chats cost more and drift.
3. For anything important, get a second opinion: paste the result into a fresh chat and ask it to find the problems.
