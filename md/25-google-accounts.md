# All Your Google Accounts in One Claude Code (P18)

<p class="tagline">No more logging in and out. Emailed Sep 8, 2026. Added Oct 14, 2026.</p>

Needs Claude Code on a Mac or Linux; the claude.ai website can't run it. About 30 minutes for two or three accounts. Paste this into Claude Code:

> Fetch https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marianasmall/mariana-google-mcp/main/SETUP-PROMPT.md and follow the instructions in it.

The guide waits for you at each Google console checkpoint. Give each account a short name. Next morning, say "sweep my inboxes."

**The one step people skip:** publish your Google app to "production." Skip it and Google logs you out every seven days.

It cannot send and it cannot delete: drafts only, and a "To Be Deleted" label for you to confirm. For Drive, install Google Drive for desktop and sign in to every account; Claude Code reads each one as a folder.
