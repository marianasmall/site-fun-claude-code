# Ask Claude About Your Meetings (P13)

<p class="tagline">Search your recordings instead of replaying them. Added Oct 10, 2026.</p>

If your meeting recorder or note-taking app has a Claude connector, Claude can find recordings, read transcripts, and read the app's own summary and action items. It reads only; it can't delete or change anything.

1. Make sure the recorder is signed in to your own account with cloud sync on.
2. In claude.ai, go to **Customize > Connectors**, find your recorder or notes app, click **Connect**, and sign in.
3. Verify (not optional):

> List my recent recordings, with the date and title of each.

Then: "Summarize the newest one and list the action items." An empty list means a different account or sync is off.
4. While you record, say instructions out loud with a fixed prefix ("for the bots: ...") and later ask Claude to find them.

Then ask real questions: who owes what, what was decided, what you promised.

It also works in Claude Code with nothing to install; each person connects their own account.

**Caveats:** the transcript is the recorder's cleaned-up version: good for summaries and action items, not word-for-word quotes, and names get mangled in noisy rooms. Check any name before you email it. On Team and Enterprise plans an admin may need to turn the connector on first. Not every recorder has a connector.

**Habit:** say a phrase out loud while you record ("for Claude: remind me to send the deck") so those lines are easy to find later.
