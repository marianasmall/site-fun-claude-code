# A Morning Brief Waiting for You (P14)

<p class="tagline">One short read instead of five tabs. Added Oct 10, 2026.</p>

Connect Google Calendar and Gmail (claude.ai: Customize > Connectors; Claude Code: `/mcp`), then paste:

> Check my calendar for today and give me a short morning brief: 1) My schedule for today, in order, with times. 2) My top 3 priorities, pulled from my calendar and from anything urgent in my unread email. 3) Emails that need a reply: only messages from real people I know or work with, not newsletters or automated notices. Keep it short enough to read in under a minute. Read only: don't send or change anything.

**Make it a habit:** run it by hand, save it as a `/morning-brief` command, or ask Claude to schedule it with `/schedule`.

Full recipe: github.com/marianasmall/claude-code-starter-kit (docs/automation-recipes/01-morning-brief.md)
