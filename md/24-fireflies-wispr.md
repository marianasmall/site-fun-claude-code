# Search Your Meeting Transcripts and Dictation Notes (P17)

<p class="tagline">Fireflies and Wispr Flow, connected to Claude. Emailed Aug 7, 2026. Added Oct 14, 2026.</p>

With Fireflies connected, Claude can search and read your meeting transcripts and summaries. With Wispr Flow connected, it can read your meeting notes and scratchpad.

In claude.ai, go to Customize > Connectors, find each one and click Connect. You sign in once and you're done. On a Team plan an admin can add a connector for the organization, but each person authorizes their own account. Then paste:

> Summarize my last meeting and list what we decided.

**Running Claude for a team?** Announce every setup change in an email whose subject starts with [Claude Update]. Searching that phrase gives everyone the full history.
