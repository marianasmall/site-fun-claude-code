# Plan Mode (P6)

<p class="tagline">See the plan before anything happens. Added Aug 7, 2026.</p>

One keystroke makes Claude outline what it's about to do — and wait for your OK — before touching a single file. Press **Shift+Tab** until the plan-mode indicator shows, or type `/plan`.

**Works even without the keystroke — paste:**

> Before doing anything, show me your full plan and wait for my approval.

**When it earns its keep:** anything scary — deleting, reorganizing, touching many files, changing something that works.

The three modes (Shift+Tab cycles): Manual (asks each step, default) · Plan first (approve once, then it executes) · Auto-accept (just goes; for fully-trusted work). Habit: plan mode for the irreversible, auto-accept for the routine, manual in between.

Docs: code.claude.com/docs/en/permission-modes
